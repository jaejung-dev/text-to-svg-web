Reward-model probe

Fine-tuned HPSv2.1 scorer, stress-tested on 1,000 OmniSVG prompts.

We took the LoRA-fine-tuned HPSv2.1 LicaScore checkpoint and scored both the human ground-truth SVG and the V19 Best-of-8 generated SVG for every prompt. The goal was not a benchmark number but to surface early weaknesses and biases in the reward model.

Takeaway

It reliably ranks ground-truth above generated — with a few verified biases.

Multi-model comparison

All 5 deployed scorers, side by side

We re-scored every prompt's ground-truth and generated SVG with the deployed lica-svg-imscore stack — HPSv2.1, PickScore, CLIPScore, ImageReward, and LAION aesthetic — to see whether the biases are HPS-specific or shared.
